Meet Ryan

Born and raised in the rural Midwest, Ryan found peace where others saw frustration. Tearing apart old toys, screwing around with tech, and exploring the unknown defined his childhood. This theme persisted into his teen and young adult years, in the form of learning to mod video games, going into web development, graphic design, and now, photography.

Who Am I?

My name is Ryan. I was born in a small town in Michigan, called Fremont. For the majority of my school years, I attended my local school, White Cloud Public Schools. During my 11th and 12th grades, I studied graphic communications at a local career-tech center, learning software and practicing concepts such as color theory, practical design theories, the Adobe creative suite, physical printing processes, among others. In addition, I have the ability of correlating my knowledge into other fields, creating a unique fieldset derived around learning the basics and adapting those basics into results.
